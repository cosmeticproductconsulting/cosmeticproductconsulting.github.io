FAQ

Cosmetic Product Development FAQs

What does a cosmetic product development consultant do?

A cosmetic product development consultant provides technical guidance across formulation, ingredient selection, prototype development, testing, manufacturing and product development.

What does a cosmetic formulation consultant do?

A cosmetic formulation consultant focuses on developing and optimising cosmetic formulas, including ingredient selection, formulation strategy, product characteristics and troubleshooting.

Can startups work with a cosmetic formulation consultant?

Yes. Startups can seek formulation consulting at the concept stage, during prototype development, while evaluating manufacturers or when preparing products for commercial production.

What is the difference between formulation and product development?

Formulation focuses on the composition and technical characteristics of a product. Product development is broader and may include concept development, formulation, testing, packaging, manufacturing and scale-up.

Can an existing cosmetic formula be improved?

Existing formulations can be evaluated for potential optimisation relating to performance, stability, compatibility, manufacturing or other development objectives.
